## The client journey — DRAFT, NEEDS REVIEW

Moved here from Data Sharing on Bob's instruction: the journey is a Joint
Eligibility story (the what and why), not a Data Sharing one.

Fictional persona. Adapt with a different name. Illustration, not a real person.

**Without joint eligibility:**
1. Applies for medication assistance at Agency A. Proof of diagnosis, income,
   residency.
2. Applies for care services at the same agency. All three again.
3. Loses his job. Updates income in two systems.
4. Applies for housing at Agency C. All three a third time.
5. Recertifies with three systems.

**With joint eligibility:**
1. One unified application, all three once.
2. Later agencies look up eligibility already verified.
3. Income updated in one system.
4. One recertification.

Three applications and three recertifications become one of each.
